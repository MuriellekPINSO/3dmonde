    Game mode

    Zém Run

    Learn the neighbourhoods riding a zémidjan or aboard a tokpa-tokpa, on the real streets of Cotonou and Calavi. Pull in at the stops, dodge the potholes, grab the coins.

    Two playersPlay together on the same line: create a game and send the link to your friend.

    ↑ accelerate (the zém only moves while you hold it, and slows down as soon as you let go) · ↓ brake · stop at red lights, or the police will fine you · ask your passenger if they have a helmet · ← → change lanes, → → pull over onto the roadside; at a junction, slow down then ← or → to turn, T to go straight on where the GPS wanted to turn: you choose, and it recalculates the route · the mini-map and the place names on screen help you find your way · E interact with what's next to you (fuel, street vendor, MoMo, tyre repairer, shops, people, a customer waving you down) · space jump · H horn · 1 2 3 answer · M weather · Esc pause. Parked with no passenger? Wait: a passer-by will come and ask you for a ride.
    Controller : RT throttle · LT brake · stick or D-pad: lanes, up: straight on · A jump · B horn · X or Y interact · during a dialogue A, B, X answer · Start pause · Select weather.
